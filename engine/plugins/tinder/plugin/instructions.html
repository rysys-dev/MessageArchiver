Tinder

A release candidate. This plug-in was built from what is known of Tinder’s download, without a real one to check it against. If something is missing or looks wrong, please tell us at rysys.net.

This plug-in lets MessageArchiver read the messages you sent on Tinder, beside Messages.

Only your side. Tinder’s download holds only the messages you sent: the other person’s words are their own data, and Tinder gives them only to them. Each conversation in your archive opens with a line saying so. The other person isn’t named in the download either.

Bring your Tinder messages in

  1. In a browser, go to account.gotinder.com/data, sign in, and choose Download My Information. Enter your email and submit. Tinder emails a link, usually within a few days.
  2. Download the file from Tinder’s email into Downloads (the link may expire soon), and open it to unzip it.
  3. In MessageArchiver, choose Tinder: Choose an Export… from the source menu (or Choose… in Settings ▸ Sources), and choose Downloads, or the unzipped folder.

What is read

Every match’s messages you sent, with GIFs and Tinder’s other kinds of message noted. Each match is a conversation called “Tinder match” and its number. Tinder numbers matches afresh in each download, so a later download can put a match under another number; no message is added twice.

Later downloads

Download again the same way, unzip it beside the earlier one, and update your archive. Only what is new is added.

Keep the download safe

The download holds your messages unencrypted, and the details of your account. Once your archive is made, you may want to move it somewhere safe, or delete it.

What it can’t do

It reads only: nothing in Tinder or the download is changed, and it has no network access at all. A GIF or voice note in the download is a web address on Tinder’s servers; it is kept as a link and never fetched.
