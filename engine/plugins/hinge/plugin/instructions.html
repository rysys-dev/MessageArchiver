Hinge

A release candidate. This plug-in was built from what is known of Hinge’s download, without a real one to check it against. If something is missing or looks wrong, please tell us at rysys.net.

This plug-in lets MessageArchiver read the messages you sent on Hinge, beside Messages.

Only your side. Hinge’s download holds only the messages you sent: the other person’s words are their own data, and Hinge gives them only to them. Each conversation in your archive opens with a line saying so. The other person isn’t named in the download either.

Bring your Hinge messages in

  1. In Hinge: your profile ▸ Settings ▸ Download My Data. Choose your country, confirm your email, and submit. Hinge emails a link when it is ready (it can take up to 30 days), and the link lasts 48 hours.
  2. Download the file into Downloads, and open it to unzip it.
  3. In MessageArchiver, choose Hinge: Choose an Export… from the source menu (or Choose… in Settings ▸ Sources), and choose Downloads, or the unzipped folder.

What is read

For each match: your like and its comment, when you matched, the messages and voice notes you sent, whether you told Hinge you met, and when the match ended. A conversation is called “Hinge match from” the date it began. Hinge’s newer downloads (CSV files) can include the other person’s messages; when they do, they are read too, and the line about only your side isn’t added.

Later downloads

Download again the same way, unzip it beside the earlier one, and update your archive. Only what is new is added.

Keep the download safe

The download holds your messages unencrypted, and the details of your account. Once your archive is made, you may want to move it somewhere safe, or delete it.

What it can’t do

It reads only: nothing in Hinge or the download is changed, and it has no network access at all. A GIF or voice note in the download is a web address on Hinge’s servers; it is kept as a link and never fetched.
