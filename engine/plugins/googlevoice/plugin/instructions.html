Google Voice

A release candidate. This plug-in was built from what is known of Google Voice’s download, without a real one to check it against. If something is missing or looks wrong, please tell us at rysys.net.

This plug-in lets MessageArchiver read your Google Voice texts, calls and voicemails, beside Messages. Google Voice names people by their phone numbers, so a person you text on both is one conversation, each message marked with how it was sent.

Bring your Google Voice messages in

Google keeps your Voice history on its servers, so you ask Google for a copy with Google Takeout. It takes from a few minutes to a few hours, and Google emails you when it is ready.

  1. Go to takeout.google.com, signed in to the Google account your Voice number is on.
  2. Press Deselect all, then tick Voice alone.
  3. Press Next step. Choose Send download link via email, Export once, .zip, and press Create export.
  4. When Google’s email arrives, download the file (or every part, for a large one) into Downloads, and open each to unzip it. A folder takeout- and the date appears.
  5. In MessageArchiver, choose Google Voice: Choose an Export… from the source menu (or Choose… in Settings ▸ Sources), and choose Downloads: every Takeout download in it is read together. You can choose the unzipped folder instead.

Later downloads

Export again the same way, unzip it beside the earlier one, and update your archive. Only what is new is added.

What is read

Texts and group texts, with the pictures, videos and contact cards sent in them; calls (incoming, outgoing and missed) as events in the conversation with that person; voicemails and recorded calls with their recordings, and Google’s transcript as a note beside them (it is Google’s hearing of the message, not its exact words). The Spam folder is left out. Google keeps no reactions, replies or edits in the download. A file Google left out is listed as missing.

Keep the download safe

The download holds your messages and voicemails unencrypted. Once your archive is made, you may want to move it somewhere safe, or delete it.

What it can’t do

It reads only: nothing in Google Voice or the download is changed, and it has no network access at all.
