WhatsApp

This plug-in lets MessageArchiver read your WhatsApp chats, beside Messages. A person you talk to on both is one conversation, each message marked with how it was sent.

On this Mac

  1. Install WhatsApp for Mac and link it to your phone (WhatsApp ▸ Settings ▸ Linked devices on the phone).
  2. Open MessageArchiver. WhatsApp shows in the source menu under Also read, ticked.

WhatsApp on a Mac holds only what it received after it was linked, and attachments only once they have been opened on the Mac. The rest are listed as missing.

What is read

One-to-one chats and groups. Status updates (24-hour posts) and broadcast lists are left out. Someone WhatsApp knows only by a hidden id, not a phone number, is listed by that id and isn’t matched to Contacts; you can join them to the right person by hand in People.

What it can’t do

It reads only: nothing in WhatsApp is changed, and it has no network access at all.
