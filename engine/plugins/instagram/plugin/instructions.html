Instagram

A release candidate. This plug-in was built from what is known of Instagram’s download, without a real one to check it against. If something is missing or looks wrong, please tell us at rysys.net.

This plug-in lets MessageArchiver read your Instagram direct messages, beside Messages.

Bring your Instagram messages in

Instagram keeps your messages on its servers, so you ask Meta for a copy, a download of your information. Meta takes from a few minutes to a few days to prepare it, and emails you when it is ready.

  1. Open Accounts Center (in Instagram: your profile ▸ the menu ▸ Accounts Center, or accountscenter.instagram.com in a browser) ▸ Your information and permissions ▸ Download your information.
  2. Choose Download or transfer information, your Instagram account, then Some of your information, and tick Messages.
  3. Choose Download to device. Set Date range to All time, Format to JSON (HTML can’t be read), and Media quality to High. Press Create files.
  4. When Meta’s email arrives, download the file (or every part, for a large one) into Downloads, and open each to unzip it.
  5. In MessageArchiver, choose Instagram: Choose an Export… from the source menu (or Choose… in Settings ▸ Sources), and choose Downloads: every part is read together. You can choose the unzipped folder instead.

Later downloads

Download again the same way, unzip it beside the earlier one, and update your archive. Only what is new is added.

What is read

Every conversation in the download, including message requests, with photos, videos, voice messages, reactions and the posts and links shared. Meta’s download names people only by the name they show on Instagram, with no phone number or email, so they aren’t matched to your Contacts on their own: you can join someone to the right person by hand in People. Meta keeps no replies or edits in the download. A file Meta left out is listed as missing.

Keep the download safe

The download holds your messages unencrypted, and the details of your account. Once your archive is made, you may want to move it somewhere safe, or delete it.

What it can’t do

It reads only: nothing in Instagram or the download is changed, and it has no network access at all.
