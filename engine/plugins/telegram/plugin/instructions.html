Telegram

This plug-in lets MessageArchiver read your Telegram chats, beside Messages. A person you talk to on both is one conversation, each message marked with how it was sent.

Bring your Telegram chats in

Telegram keeps your chats on its servers, so you ask Telegram for a copy, its data export. Telegram may ask you to confirm on your phone, and the first time it can make you wait about a day before the export is ready.

Telegram for Mac (the App Store app): Settings ▸ Data and Storage ▸ Export Telegram Data. Telegram Desktop: Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Export Telegram data. In either:

  1. Tick the chats you want (personal chats, groups) and the photos, videos, voice messages and files to include.
  2. Set Format to Machine-readable JSON, or Both. The plug-in reads the JSON; HTML alone can’t be read.
  3. Set Folder to Downloads. Telegram for Mac otherwise saves inside its own folder in Library, where MessageArchiver can’t look.
  4. Press Export. A folder appears in Downloads (Telegram_Export_ and the date, or DataExport_ and the date in Telegram Desktop’s folder).
  5. In MessageArchiver, choose Telegram: Choose an Export… from the source menu (or Choose… in Settings ▸ Sources), and choose that folder, or Downloads itself: the newest export in it is read.

Later exports

Export again the same way and update your archive. Only what is new is added.

What is read

One-to-one chats, groups, bots and Saved Messages, with replies, reactions, calls and group changes. Channels are left out. Telegram’s export has phone numbers only in your contacts: a person whose name matches one of your contacts gets that number, so they are matched to Contacts and Messages; anyone else is listed by Telegram’s id with their name, and you can join them to the right person by hand in People. Telegram keeps only the last version of an edited message, so it is marked edited without its earlier versions. A file left out of the export (too large, or its kind not ticked) is listed as missing.

Keep the export safe

The export folder holds your messages unencrypted, and the details of your account. Once your archive is made, you may want to move the export somewhere safe, or delete it.

What it can’t do

It reads only: nothing in Telegram or the export is changed, and it has no network access at all.
