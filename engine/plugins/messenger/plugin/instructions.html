Messenger

A release candidate. This plug-in was built from what is known of Meta’s downloads, without a real one to check it against. If something is missing or looks wrong, please tell us at rysys.net.

This plug-in lets MessageArchiver read your Facebook Messenger chats, beside Messages.

Bring your Messenger chats in

Messenger keeps your chats on Meta’s servers, in two places, so you download two things. Most chats since 2024 are end-to-end encrypted and are only in the second; the first has the history from before.

1. Your Facebook information. Meta takes from a few minutes to a few days to prepare it, and emails you when it is ready.

  1. Open Accounts Center (accountscenter.facebook.com) ▸ Your information and permissions ▸ Download your information.
  2. Choose Download or transfer information, your Facebook account, then Some of your information, and tick Messages.
  3. Choose Download to device. Set Date range to All time, Format to JSON (HTML can’t be read), and Media quality to High. Press Create files.
  4. When Meta’s email arrives, download the file (or every part) into Downloads, and open each to unzip it.

2. Your encrypted chats. On a computer, at facebook.com or messenger.com: Messenger’s Settings ▸ Privacy & safety ▸ End-to-end encrypted chats ▸ Message storage (or Chat history) ▸ Download secure storage data (or Download chat backup). Message storage must be turned on. Save the file in Downloads and open it to unzip it.

Then, in MessageArchiver, choose Messenger: Choose an Export… from the source menu (or Choose… in Settings ▸ Sources), and choose Downloads: both downloads are read together, and a chat in both is one conversation, each message once.

Later downloads

Download again the same way, unzip beside the earlier ones, and update your archive. Only what is new is added.

What is read

Every chat (the inbox, archived chats, message requests), with photos, videos, voice messages, files, reactions, shared links and calls. Meta’s downloads name people only by the name they show on Facebook, with no phone number or email, so they aren’t matched to your Contacts on their own: you can join someone to the right person by hand in People. A group chat that moved to encryption may show as two conversations, one from each download. Meta keeps no replies or edits in the downloads. A file Meta left out is listed as missing.

Keep the downloads safe

The downloads hold your messages unencrypted, and the details of your account. Once your archive is made, you may want to move them somewhere safe, or delete them.

What it can’t do

It reads only: nothing in Messenger or the downloads is changed, and it has no network access at all.
